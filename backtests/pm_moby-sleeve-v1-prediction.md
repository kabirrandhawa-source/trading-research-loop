# Prediction: Moby picks slice (hypothesis 13)

Written 2026-10-15, before the first pick (Coeur Mining, CDE) entered the slice.

## The test (fixed in advance)
- $10,000 paper slice. Each Moby pick Kabir records gets a fixed 20% at the next open (newest 5 active at
  most), held until Moby says sell, its price target is reached, or 26 weeks pass. Code applies the rule
  (sleeve.picks); no Claude judgment. Orders go through the kill switch like every slice.
- Forward test from 2026-10-15 for 26 weeks (to about 2027-04-15), scored against SPY's price change over
  the same days. Idle cash counts: if Moby sends few picks, the slice is mostly cash, which is part of
  what "following Moby" really earns.

## What I expect
1. The slice does not beat SPY: excess return between -8 and +3 percentage points over 26 weeks.
2. Higher volatility and a deeper drawdown than SPY (single stocks, small-cap miners like CDE).
3. Moby's "share of picks profitable" will look fine (over half) even if the slice lags SPY: that number
   ignores what SPY did over the same holding period.

## What counts as a pass
Beats SPY by at least 3 percentage points over the 26 weeks, with at least 6 picks taken. Even then it is
one 26-week sample; a pass earns a second period, not real money.
